# Project plan

## Project Background

The main challenge is that temperature and humidity can change quickly and must be monitored to keep the growing environment at suitable levels.

The intended users may not be plant experts, so the measurements and any resulting analysis should be easy to understand.

The internal climate is affected by supporting electrical equipment. This equipment can quickly change the temperature and humidity, so it is important to detect unsuitable conditions before they become harmful.

### Existing solutions

- [Hydroponics research article](https://www.mdpi.com/2673-4591/122/1/3)

- [Hydro0x01 project](https://github.com/40rbidd3n/Hydro0x01)

IoT and embedded systems make it possible to collect, process and analyse sensor data. In future versions, the collected data could also support automated control that helps maintain suitable growing conditions.

## Purpose

The purpose is to build a prototype with sensors that produce accurate, stable and reliable measurements. The project will also work towards a robust system that can analyse measurements and support decisions based on historical data.

## Project Goals

- Measure the air temperature and relative humidity inside the growing environment.

- Measure the air temperature outside the growing environment.

- Measure the water or nutrient-solution temperature.

## Project Organisation

All team members share responsibility for the project rather than having one permanent role. Tasks are managed in Trello. When a team member completes a task, they select the next prioritised task from the backlog.
